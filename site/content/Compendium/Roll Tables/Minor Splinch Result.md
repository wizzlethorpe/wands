# Minor Splinch Result

A minor splinch is when the lost body part is generally non-threatening; perhaps an eyebrow, a part of your finger, or a small section of skin. You determine how much slashing damage you suffer by rolling a single [[/r d10]]. This damage cannot be reduced or prevented in any way.
