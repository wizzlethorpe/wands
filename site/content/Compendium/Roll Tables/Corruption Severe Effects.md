# Corruption Severe Effects

Experimenting with Dark magic almost always results from an unquenchable thirst for power, an incredibly intense fear or a deep well of hatred. By using twisted magic and committing unspeakable acts, that inner darkness takes a toll on a Dark witch's physical and mental well-being.
