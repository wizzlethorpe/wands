# The Third Eye

You can see things hidden from most mortals. You gain truesight, the ability to see in normal and magical darkness, see invisible creatures and objects, automatically detect visual illusions and succeed on saving throws against them, perceive the original form of a shapechanger or a creature that is transformed by magic, and see into the Ethereal Plane, all out to a range of 120 feet.
