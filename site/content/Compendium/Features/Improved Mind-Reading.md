# Improved Mind-Reading

You can see surface thoughts with nothing more than a glance. You can use an action to cast [[Legilimens]] targeting one humanoid you can see within 60 feet. That creature does not receive a saving throw against this use of Legilimency, but you may only obtain their surface thoughts.
