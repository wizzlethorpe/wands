# Loyal Beast

You've tamed a magical beast that serves as your companion. Choose a beast with a challenge rating of 1/4 or lower. The beast obeys your commands and acts on your initiative. As an action, you can command it to take the Attack, Dash, Disengage, Dodge, or Help action.
