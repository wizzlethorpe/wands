# Total Recall

*Improved Mind-Reading required.* You've achieved complete mastery over the mind arts. You can uncover thoughts hidden in the deepest of minds and implant suggestions in the shallowest of souls. When you cast [[Legilimens]] or [[Imperius]], you can do so non-verbally and without eye contact. Your range is the distance in which you can see your target and is not limited by eye contact.
