# Total Legilimency

*Improved Legilimency required.* Once you get into a target's head, you know its deepest secrets. When you cast [[Legilimens]], you may choose to cast it at a higher level (consuming the appropriate spell slot). If the target fails their saving throw, you can delve deeper into their memories.
