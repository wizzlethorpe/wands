# Melofors

You conjure a pumpkin around a target's head, blinding and deafening it. Choose one creature that you can see within range to make a Wisdom saving throw. If it fails, the target is blinded and deafened for the duration. At the end of each of its turns, the target can make a Wisdom saving throw. On a success, the spell ends.

***At Higher Levels***. When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 4th level or higher, you can target one additional creature for each slot level above 3rd.
