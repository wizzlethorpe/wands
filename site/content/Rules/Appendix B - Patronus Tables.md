---

Your character's corporeal patronus can be determined several different ways:

1. **Choose your patronus.** Its form makes no significant mechanical impact on the [[Expecto Patronum|expecto patronum]] spell.
2. **Roll on the d100 table** below for a random result.
3. **Use customized tables** based on your character's Background and House.

If your character is not a Hogwarts student, choose a house based on your character's personality and values.

> **Note:** Results 91-00 on the main table are magical beasts. If you are determining your [[Chapter 3 - Schools of Magic#Your Animagus Form|animagus form]], you must re-roll if you get a magical beast result.
