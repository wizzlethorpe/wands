A popular dueling spell among young wizards, this jinx makes a being's fingers numb and relaxed. Make a ranged spell attack against a creature within range. On a hit, the target has disadvantage on attack rolls for the duration. At the start of each of its turns, the target can make a Dexterity saving throw. On a success, the spell ends.
